## What Is Actually Happening

[Explain the mechanism. Name the system, incentives, constraints, or failure mode. Use concrete nouns.]

[Give the strongest objection. Do not strawman it.]

## Section Patterns

Use these headings as needed:

- `## The Default View`
- `## The Missing Constraint`
- `## What Changed My Mind`
- `## Why This Feels Wrong`
- `## The Boring Version That Works`
- `## Where This Breaks`
- `## What I Would Do`

## Title Patterns

Good title shapes:

- `X Is Not Y`
- `The Boring Way To Do X`
- `Why X Fails In Practice`
- `The Part Of X Nobody Wants To Own`
- `Do X Before You Build Y`
- `X Is A Trust Boundary, Not A Breakthrough`

Avoid titles that only say:

- `Thoughts On X`
- `A Few Notes About X`
- `The Future Of X`
- `Why X Matters`
